# Domain and DNS

Custom-domain work is deliberately deferred. Do not modify website, email, verification, or subdomain records during foundation work.

Before launch, separately document the registrar, DNS provider, root and `www` domains, email provider, MX, SPF, DKIM, DMARC, verification records, subdomains, original values, and rollback plan. The Squarespace site must remain available until the new site is approved and stable.
