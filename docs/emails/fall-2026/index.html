Wayfinders on the Hudson
Fall 2026

Come home to Wayfinders this fall.

Summer is still here, and we hope you are soaking it up. But before long, the days will shorten, school will begin, calendars will fill, and life will start moving faster.

At Wayfinders, autumn offers another invitation: stay close to the natural world, deepen your relationships, and make room for what matters.

Featured This Fall
For Kids

Base Camp

Adventure. Confidence. Outdoor skills.

Afternoons are for screen-free outdoor time: building fires and forts, scavenger hunts, foraging and cooking wild foods, field games, earth art, Taekwondo, and ecological restoration.

Every Base Camp child also becomes part of Restoration Nation, caring for the Hudson Valley through hands-on restoration and community stewardship.

Grades 1–8: Tuesdays + Wednesdays, Sept. 22–Dec. 9, 3:45–5:45 PM
Kindergarten: Thursdays, Sept. 24–Dec. 3, 3:45–5 PM
Location: Lenoir Preserve, Yonkers

For Women

Autumn Women’s Retreat

Rest. Renewal. A return to what matters.

Gather around the fire, slow down, and reconnect with yourself, community, and the earth. We’ll begin with a somatic warm-up and then move into a choice of sessions exploring Vision Quest, Nature Genograms, or Ancient Goddess Cultures for Modern Women.

Friday, September 18
1:30–5:30 PM · Lenoir Preserve · $120
